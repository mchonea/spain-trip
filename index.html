💡 Good to know
  • Pickpockets: La Rambla, the Barcelona metro and Madrid's Sol area are the hot spots. Front pockets, zipped bags, phones off café tables.
  • Alhambra: Nasrid Palace entry is strict to the minute and names on tickets are checked against ID. Bring passports and arrive early.
  • Park Güell: the Monumental Zone (mosaic lizard, serpentine bench) needs a timed ticket; the rest of the park is free.
  • London overnight: leaving Heathrow for a hotel means clearing UK border control. US citizens need a UK ETA, applied for online in advance.
  • Spanish hours: lunch runs 2–4 PM and dinner rarely starts before 8:30–9 PM. Many small shops close mid-afternoon.
